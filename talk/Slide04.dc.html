Method
SPPT perturbs parametrisations. A network has weights.
W̃m  =  W  ⊙  ( 1 + σ ξm )
ξm,i ~ N(0, 1) i.i.d., one draw per member, per tensor
Multiplicative, not additive
Every scalar weight carries variance σ2|w|2, so σ is a dimensionless relative strength and the trained weight distribution is preserved. As in Gaussian dropout.
Frozen across the rollout
Member m draws ξm once and reuses it for every autoregressive step. Each member is a fixed weight-space neighbour of the checkpoint.
Two degrees of freedom
The selection set S (which tensors to perturb) and the magnitude σ. Everything in this talk is a sweep over those two.
Weight-space ensembling: rather than averaging many fine-tuned checkpoints as in model soups, we sample weight-space neighbours of one checkpoint and ensemble their forecasts.
