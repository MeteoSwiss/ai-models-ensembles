Conclusions
Take-aways
01
A frozen deterministic checkpoint already carries a usable ensemble: 0.04 to 0.13 CRPSS behind the best trained model at 240 h, at zero marginal training cost.
02
The injection site is architecture-specific. Budget a sweep per checkpoint; start at all weights, σ = 0.01 to 0.03.
03
The failure is the domain mean, and it belongs to the perturbation target: scale-restrict the noise, or add perturbed initial conditions.
04
Which ensemble for which job: trained-probabilistic where it covers your variables, SPW for the checkpoints and centres it does not.
Next: choose the injection site by Fisher information instead of searching it; steer the draw rather than randomise it; carry the framework to limited-area models.
