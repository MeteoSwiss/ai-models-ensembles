Limitations
What this does not show
Sweep cost
Every ablation cell is a full 10-member run. The per-checkpoint configuration search is the price paid for the absent training run.
No within-family baseline
MC-dropout and SWAG need training access the frozen public checkpoints lack, so a fair intra-family comparison is out of reach.
Sampling uncertainty
The 112 inits are twice-daily within eight month-blocks, so the effective sample size is closer to eight than to 112.
M = 10, not 50
Enough to support the ranking, but the 10-member SSR and CRPSS remain biased in absolute terms.
An external IC ensemble
The IC augmentation draws from the IFS-ENS EDA. Where none is available, cruder substitutes need not transfer the calibration.
No precipitation
Humidity, the most non-Gaussian field we do score, is already the worst calibrated. Perturbing a smoothed solution adds spread but cannot restore sharpness the parent never produced.
