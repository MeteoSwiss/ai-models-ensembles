The gap
Skill without spread
Deterministic MLWMs now match or beat the IFS at 0.25°, at a small fraction of the inference cost.
But a single forecast carries no estimate of its own uncertainty, and the atmosphere is chaotic. Operational use needs a distribution.
The obvious answer is to train a probabilistic model. That costs a dedicated training run, and assumes you can retrain at all.
How much calibrated uncertainty can we recover from a deterministic checkpoint that already exists and cannot be retrained?
Because the training data, the compute, or the code are out of reach.
