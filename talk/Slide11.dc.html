Q2 · interpretation
What the winners share: proximity to the low-resolution latent
Aurora's encoder produces the Perceiver latent. AIFS's decoder reads the O96 processor mesh. SFNO's ℓ < 10 is the coarse scale by construction. Each borders the low-resolution stage rather than the full-resolution interior.
Perturbing the interior underdisperses: Aurora's backbone and the central processors of AIFS and SFNO carry normalisation layers that damp the injected variance.
Not the trained site: AIFS-ENS injects its learned noise in exactly the processor we cannot exploit.
GraphCast falls outside the pattern entirely.
The honest caveat
Four architectures cannot establish a rule. Independent latent-space work finds the same coarse-to-fine organisation in GraphCast and Aurora, which makes this a hypothesis worth testing on more models.
What to do on an unseen checkpoint
Start with all weights at σ = 0.01 to 0.03, then move the perturbation onto the stage bordering the low-resolution latent, where one exists.
