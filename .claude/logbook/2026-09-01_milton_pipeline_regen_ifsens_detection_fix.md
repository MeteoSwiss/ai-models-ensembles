## The IFS-ENS detection-rate error
Published table: IFS-ENS detection rate 71% (140-pair denominator, same as every other row). Fresh
regeneration: 19/140 = 14%. Investigated rather than just taking the new number:
- Found the *original* ifs_ens tracking job, 2473058 (predates the purge by months, ran against
  whatever `mch/s83/IFS/ifs_ens.zarr` resolved to at the time, on the now-deleted `venvs/milton_case`).
  Its raw log: 88/700 full-ensemble detections, 17/140 on the same stratified-10 subset used everywhere
  else - i.e. ~12%, not 71%, from the very first run.
- `[[ifs-ens-10m-wind-nan-bug]]` (`.claude/logbook/ifs_ens_10m_wind_nan_bug.md`, written 2026-06-06)
  independently states "the 13% IFS-ENS Milton detection rate" was what the paper had *at that time* -
  so 13% (not 71%) was the historically-correct, already-documented number. Something between 06-06 and
  the table's "Regenerated 2026-06-11" header replaced it with 71%, and it survived every review pass
  from 06-21 through 06-28 unchallenged.
- Tested and ruled out four alternative explanations before concluding it's just wrong:
  1. a122->ab016 store migration: byte-identical zarr metadata both sides, not the cause.
  2. NaN-gap-driven ("unfair to score NaN as no-detection" - user's hypothesis): max consecutive dead
     leads is 6-12h everywhere, under the tracker's own 18h bridge; NaN fraction (4-41%) does not
     correlate with which inits actually detect - the two cleanest inits by NaN fraction (4%, 6.7% dead)
     detect *nothing*, while two noisier ones (26%, 11% dead) detect nearly the whole ensemble.
  3. ERA5-vs-IBTrACS row-count ratio mix-up (user's second hypothesis): matched-vs-ERA5 / matched-vs-
     IBTrACS ratios are 0.91-1.10, nowhere near 0.71.
  4. Alternative "detection" definitions: per-member-across-any-init gives 100% (task 12 alone detects
     all 50 members), per-init-any-member gives 14% (2 of 14 inits have any hit at all) - neither is 71%.
  `analyze_milton_stats.py`/`aggregate_tracks.py` were never committed to git, so there is no diff to
  recover the original computation from. Provenance of 71% is untraceable; 13-14% is reproduced by two
  independent runs eight months apart plus the contemporaneous memory. Treated 71% as the error.
