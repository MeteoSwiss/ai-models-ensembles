  MSL +1.9%, Z500 +3.9%, T500 +1.3%, U850 +0.9%. Data in /tmp/p6c_crps_neutrality.txt
  (volatile; numbers preserved here).
- AIFS 4-init design grid, spatial-mean SSR @240h: variable-median 2.9 (frozen) -> 1.8

- AIFS 4-init CRPS deltas frozen -> p6c: T2m +2.4%, MSL -0.6%, Z500 +4.9%, T500 +4.1%,
  U850 -1.8% (mixed, within ~5%).
- Two-point sigma probe (production sigma vs 1.4x) selected UNCHANGED production sigma
